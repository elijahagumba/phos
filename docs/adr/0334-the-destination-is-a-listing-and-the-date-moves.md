## Context

Elijah stated the goal on 27 October 2026: a company in which his own stake is in the mid hundreds of
millions, reached by a public listing rather than a sale.

`docs/zero-to-one-master-plan.md`, written four days earlier, is an eight-quarter sequence whose
month-24 **pass** case is one plant reading eight sentences in a shadow week, with Intelligence, an
API and a UI "discussable only as a later programme". M3 permits the first cheque only after the
month-12 gate. §10 forbids a product surface until after a staffable shadow.

Those two statements are incompatible, and the arithmetic is not close. A mid-hundreds stake at a
listing needs roughly $250M ARR at the multiples that have cleared since 2025, against public
enterprise SaaS at 3.3× revenue on 31 March 2026. The fastest enterprise software listing on record
is Samsara at six years from founding in an easier market; SentinelOne took eight with about $93M of
prior-year revenue; the two companies in Atlas's own market, Claroty and Dragos, took eight years to
reach $100–154M ARR and neither had listed at year ten. PHOS has no customers, no revenue, no
company, and one unrun invariant harness on an unmerged branch.

A three-year listing was therefore not a hard plan. It was not a plan. The honest options were to
change the ambition or to change the date.

## Decision

**The destination is a public listing. The target is H1 2034. The plan is
`docs/ipo-roadmap-2026-2034.md`.**

The new file separates two things the master plan had joined, and that separation is the substance of
this record:

1. **The measurement constitution is permanent.** Declarations before measurements, frozen rows,
   unappended result log, held-out splits closed, no score-word on the law page, no confidence on any
   user-facing surface, command-lead unpooled, v1 at 8 of 12 until a declared measurement moves it.
   None of it is tradeable for revenue. It is what makes the company listable: an S-1 is a disclosure
   document, and most security companies arrive at one having to unwind claims their data never
   supported.
2. **The commercial sequence changes now.** Incorporate by 31 December 2026 rather than when a
   counterparty asks. The commercial licence becomes an ADR before the first partner call rather than
   at the first invoice. Non-dilutive capital now and an equity seed in H2 2027, gate or no gate. The
   product-surface ban lifts from Q3 2027, each surface under its own ADR, with the law page's
   contract travelling into every one unchanged. Four to eight parallel shadows, paid from the second.
   A third clock, commercial, that shares no result with the residual or law clocks.

The gate is 31 October 2029 and has four conditions: revenue that recurs at $9M ARR audited from
FY2028; a product a utility can procure; a company with a CFO, a clean cap table and independent
directors; and a category position including the referee benchmark. A missing condition moves the
date in a gate ADR. It does not slide quietly.

## Consequences

**Gained.** A date the comparables support, and a sequence in which first revenue lands in 2027,
which is load-bearing because underwriters read three to four audited years. The highest-value
unbuilt asset in the tree, the referee benchmark filed under "Later, not v1" in
`docs/v1-roadmap.md` §2.3, is promoted to the first public act, because it needs no customers and is
the one asset strengthened rather than slowed by the refusals.

**Cost.** Ownership. The illustrative ladder lands Elijah near 21% at listing, against the roughly
half he would hold on a private-round path. An IPO is selling, in slices, to strangers, with a lockup
and quarterly accountability. It buys independence from any single acquirer. It does not buy not
selling, and this record says so rather than letting the word "listing" hide it.

**Risk named.** The probability of a 2034 listing, executed competently, is 5 to 10%. The largest
good outcome in the distribution is an acquisition at $300M to $1B, which Elijah has refused. That
offer will arrive around 2030 at the moment it is hardest to decline. What would and would not be
accepted should be recorded before the room, not in it. That decision is not taken here.

**Ten years.** This file should be rewritten at the 2029 gate, whichever way it goes, and deleted if
the gate stops the sequence. A listing roadmap left standing after its own gate failed would hide the
real plan the way the pre-0273 rules hid the live programme.

## What would reverse this

Weakening anything in §2 of the new plan to make a quarter work. Putting a score, a confidence, a
weight or a magnitude on a product surface because a buyer asked. Inferring a station from a function
code, or treating a function code as a download, an upload or an online edit, to make an inlet
easier. Calling a break an attack, or an empty page a safe plant, in a post or a deck. Pooling
known-station 0 of 16 with unknown-master 1 of 4 in a pitch. Claiming 10×, 2–3 alerts a day, ≥70%
command lead, or any percent ready. Appending `eval_runs/log.jsonl`, or opening a held-out split
without a committed declaration, because a deadline was close. Treating this plan as a measurement, a
detection claim or a ship authorisation. Reading "listing" as permission to start Intelligence, an
API, a UI or a TAP ahead of the ADR that declares each one.

P1 remains the name of the human conversation. This record does not unblock it. The three organisation
emails drafted on 4 October 2026 remain unsent, and no plan in this repository substitutes for sending
them.
