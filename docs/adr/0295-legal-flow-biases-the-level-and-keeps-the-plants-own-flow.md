# ADR 0295 — `legal_flow` biases the level and keeps the flow the plant would emit

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **PART:** Build brief E, lane 2, the corpus
- **Supersedes:** nothing
- **Depends on:** [ADR 0122](0122-a-phos-generated-icssim-corpus.md),
  [ADR 0285](0285-the-corpus-gains-a-coordinated-low-amplitude-class.md),
  [ADR 0294](0294-three-rescues-are-refused-and-lane-2-stays-a-question.md)

## Context

Lane 2 is untested, not refuted. ADR 0285's `creep_many` biased several instruments
independently and the frozen engine caught it on the ordinary per-signal columns, 10 of 10.
The brief then asked for an attack that moves level and flow together in the ratio the plant
holds. That ratio was checked in ICSSim at the pinned commit bcfd184, in FactorySimulation.py,
before any detector was run against this class.

The plant does not have a continuous ratio. While the output valve is open and the tank holds
water, outflow is the constant `TANK_OUTPUT_FLOW_RATE` (0.0001 litre per millisecond).
Otherwise it is 0. Level is the tank's water divided by `TANK_LEVEL_CAPACITY` (3 litres),
clamped to `TANK_MAX_LEVEL` (10). Scaling flow by the same fraction as level would report a
flow the plant cannot emit.

## Decision

**`legal_flow` reports `tank_level_value` as the true level times `1 + f`, and reports
`tank_output_flow_value` as the flow the pinned physics would emit for that reported level and
the true output-valve state.**

`f` is drawn from **0.03 to 0.08**, the instrument band ADR 0285 already fixed. It is reused
and not adjusted. The reported level is clamped to `[0, 10]`. Flow is `0.0001` when the valve
is open and the reported level still holds water, and `0` otherwise. No other tag is touched.
The champion's threshold is not in this definition.

The class is an attack, on a new scenario `mixed3`: the `mixed2` kinds plus `legal_flow`, ten
events, 10800 simulated seconds. Ten events do not fit 9000 seconds at the declared maximum
lengths. `mixed` and `mixed2` are unchanged.

New seeds, because the generator changes:

| Role | Directory | Seeds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Development | `phos-icssim-dev3` | 3 `normal` 96401–96403, 4 `mixed3` 96411–96414 |
| Held out | `phos-icssim-heldout3` | 20 `mixed3` 96501–96520 |

`phos-icssim-heldout3` is score-once and unopened. It is not generated by the command below.
Development may be iterated. The fraction band may not be changed after a detector runs.

**Command, after this record is committed:**

```
python scripts/icssim/generate_corpus.py development3
uv run python scripts/lane_e_icssim_baseline.py --corpus data/phos-icssim-dev3
```

**Bars, on that development corpus, not reworded from the brief:**

1. The breadth detector catches strictly more attacks within the 8-alert shift budget than the
   frozen engine.
2. The largest share any one signal contributes to the breadth detector's catches is below
   11 of 53.

`legal_flow` is reported on its own either way. A trivial catch is the result. It is not a
reason to change `f`.

Simulated, so diagnosis, not proof. v1 stays 8 of 12. 0277 and 0278 stay unwritten until the
first bar is met on development and a held-out declaration names `phos-icssim-heldout3`.

## Consequences

The attack the brief described as a ratio is not this plant. The attack this plant can host is
a level bias that keeps flow legal. Whether that is invisible per signal is what the command
measures, and this record does not contain that measurement.

## What would reverse this

Changing 0.03, 0.08, 0.0001, 3, or 10 after a detector has run. Opening
`phos-icssim-heldout3` without a declaration that names it. Regenerating `phos-icssim-dev`,
`phos-icssim-dev2`, `phos-icssim-heldout`, or `phos-icssim-heldout2`.
