# ADR 0327 — The front door shows the path, and labels the older engine

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-24
- **PART:** the public record
- **Supersedes:** nothing
- **Depends on:** [ADR 0324](0324-the-operating-plan-loads-in-both-tools.md),
  [ADR 0044](0044-public-surfaces-on-main-must-move-with-every-advancement.md)

## Context

`docs/zero-to-one-master-plan.md` is the sequence from 23 October 2026 to
23 October 2028. A valuation has no date in that plan. It waits on a plant
that can finish the page, a commercial licence, and a second plant with its
own register.

The README opened with the speech contract and then continued as a diary of
the older detectors. Further down, "The problem" still said, in the present
tense, that PHOS learns a model of normal operation and reports a confidence.
That is the older engine. A reader who came for the page met a second
product. The public overlay asked a plant to run alongside and send a copy
of the network traffic. The plan's ask is files the plant exports, and laws
the plant writes. PHOS does not connect to the control network.

This record adds no measurement and no code. v1 stays 8 of 12.

## Decision

**The front door shows where to read, and the older engine is labelled
before its numbers.**

Under the speech contract, `README.md` points at the page, the tests, the
one protocol, the six laws, and the plan. It says there is no screen, no
service, and no connection to a control network. It says the second year of
the plan starts only if one plant exports files and writes the laws. It says
a price for the company has no date in that plan.

The measurements stay. The heading above them says they are the older
engine, kept so a published number can be rerun. They are not the page.

`public/overlay/README.md` says the pictures are those measurements. The
plant paragraph asks for exported files and laws the site writes. It does
not ask for a copy of the control network.

### Options

1. Print a valuation on the front door. The plan says that price has no
   date. Refused.
2. Leave the diary and the present-tense confidence paragraph as the second
   thing a reader sees. A stranger cannot tell which product is the page.
   Refused.
3. Label the older engine, and put the path under the speech contract.
   Chosen. No number moves.

## Consequences

**Gained.** A reader can find the page, the six laws, and the condition on
the second year without reading the diary first. The plant ask matches the
plan.

**Cost.** This record does not score a corpus and does not start a screen,
a service, or a connection. v1 stays 8 of 12. P1 stays a human conversation.

## What would reverse this

Putting a valuation, a round, or a date for either on the front door.
Describing the older engine as the page a plant would read. Asking a plant
for a connection to the control network. Deleting the measurements or the
refusals so the diary looks quieter.
