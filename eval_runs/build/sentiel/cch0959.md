The red-team note for scenario 2 says he starts surfing job websites and soliciting
employment from a competitor, and that before leaving he uses a thumb drive, at
markedly higher rates than his previous activity, to steal data.

The label runs from 2 August 2010 10:34:31 through 30 September 2010 15:04:03.

181 device rows (92 connects and 89 disconnects). It has no logon rows and no file rows.
File copies are in `file.csv` anyway, and the trace counts them.

The scorer stamps a person-day at midnight. Midnight on 2 August is before 10:34, so
that day is not one of the 42 scored malicious days, even though the answer file starts
there with four job pages, including the first request to lockheedmartinjobs.com.

and those 42 are exactly the answer-file days after 2 August. The other 17 midnights
are weekends and Monday 6 September, which have no events.

August is the job search. From 2 August through 31 August the answer file has 112 web
requests and 7 emails, and no device rows. The pages are job sites. The emails go to
Mario.Truman.Telsa@lockheed.com. The trace shows he is also doing his ordinary day on
those dates: one logon, a couple of drive connects, a few file copies, a few other emails,
and about 60 web hosts.

September is the drive. On 1 September the answer file still has 9 job pages and also

(all 181 device rows fall in September) plus one email on 14 September to his
supervisor. The trace on those days still shows a normal-sized web day and a few emails.
