# The frozen engine on the ICSSim development corpus

**Date:** 2026-10-21 · **Development data only.** `phos-icssim-heldout` was not read.
**Not a v1 number, and not a result about a real plant** — this is a simulated plant, which R4
and ADR 0143 make diagnosis rather than proof. Nothing was tuned. `eval_runs/log.jsonl` was not
appended.

Command: `uv run python scripts/lane_e_icssim_baseline.py`

Detector: `atlas-v1-consistency` exactly as frozen. Fitted on the three `normal` runs (43,203
rows, 13 signals, 1-second sample period), scored on the ten `mixed` runs. An attack counts as
caught when a budgeted alert falls inside its own labelled window, at the frozen budget of 8
alerts per 8-hour shift.

**24 of 60 attacks caught within budget. 4 of 20 benign events also drew an alert.**

## Why this corpus is usable where the synthetic plants were not

On the synthetic harness both the frozen engine and the lane 2 variant caught 6 of 6 on every
shape, so no variant could be distinguished from the champion (lane Q hit the same wall at 20 of
20 against 20 of 20, ADR 0233). Here the frozen engine sits at 40%, with two classes at zero and
36 attacks missed. There is room for a variant to be better or worse, which is what a development
corpus has to provide.

## What the two zero classes mean for the lanes

Both are missed for a structural reason, not a tuning one, and both are reasons the command path
exists.

- **`recon`, 0 of 10.** Reconnaissance is reads only. It never moves the plant, so a
  telemetry-only detector cannot see it at all, however it is tuned. The command path can: an
  unusual read pattern from a station that does not normally poll.
- **`mimic_maintenance`, 0 of 10.** An attack whose writes come from the **operator** station,
  on the same tag and with the same values as the genuine `maintenance` event in the same run
  (`tank_output_valve_mode` to 1.0, then back to 3.0). Attribution alone cannot separate it
  because the station is legitimate; physics alone cannot, because real maintenance disturbs the
  plant too. Only the two together can — which is the claim ADR 0273 lane 1 exists to test, and
  the Stuxnet-shaped case open since ADR 0034.

**So lane 1's bar is reachable in principle.** ADR 0273 requires strictly more attacks caught
plus at least one class the frozen engine misses entirely; there are two such classes.

A third opportunity, not a bar: the benign `maintenance` event drew a false alert in 4 of 10
runs. A command-aware engine that can see the operator commanded the change, and that the plant
followed, might suppress those rather than only adding catches.

## What this does not say

It does not say Atlas is 40% on real plants, or that these six classes are representative of
anything. It is one simulated plant, generated by PHOS, scored once on its development half, to
find out whether the corpus can tell detectors apart. It can.
