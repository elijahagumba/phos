# 118. Every sentence names the law it came from

*The law's name, then the fact. A score-word is refused. ADR 0323.*

Every sentence the page can show names the declared law it came from, then
the fact that made that law held, broken, or unstated. A held sentence names
its law. A broken sentence names its law. An unstated sentence names its
law. The fact still names the tags, the station, the valve, or the act.

A sentence that does not name its law is refused. It does not take a slot.
A sentence that carries a score, a confidence, a probability, a weight, or
a magnitude is refused, and that sentence is not returned. The refusal is
the word. It is not a new threshold. A number the plant already uses may
stay in the fact when none of those words are in it.

No new law was added. The order is still time, then the law name. The page
still holds at most eight. The old detector files, the other page builders,
and the checks those laws already had were not edited. Nothing was scored
on a plant. Version 1 still stands at eight of twelve. This page is not
that result.
