# 91. The joint look caught fewer

*The new attack was generated, then scored, on the development runs only. ADR 0296.*

The level was nudged by 3 to 8 percent and the flow was set to the value the plant itself
would have shown. That was fixed before the score.

On four simulated runs the joint column caught 15 of 32 attacks. The engine we already ship
caught 16 of 32. The test asked the joint column to catch strictly more. It caught fewer.

Of the four new events, each engine caught two. The new attack is not invisible, and looking
at the instruments together did not find more of it.

The nudge stays where it was set. The sealed runs were not opened. A made-up plant is a
diagnosis, not a proof. Version 1 still stands at eight of twelve.
