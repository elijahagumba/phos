# 62 — The clean look, and Atlas loses both

The command that was written down first has now been run, once.

On the 2022 edition Atlas catches 5 of 58 attacks inside the shift budget. A range
check catches 6. After both use the same grouping rule, Atlas is still on 5 and the
range check is on 10.

On the 2023 edition Atlas catches 3 of 52. The range check catches 4. Grouping
raises Atlas to 5 and the range check to 6. The gap that remains is one attack, in
the range check's favour.

Neither edition was used to choose the rule. Nothing was adjusted after these counts.
