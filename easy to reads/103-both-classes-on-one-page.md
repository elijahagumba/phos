# 103. Both classes on one page

*The joint check misses the walk. The walk check misses the joint move. Together they fit. ADR 0308.*

Four pairs of tags, and four slow walks. The joint check catches the four pairs and
misses the four walks. The walk check does the opposite. Either way, four are missed.

Put both on one page of eight and all eight are there. The misses go from four to
none. A single ordinary write still adds nothing.

This is a made example, not a plant. Version 1 still stands at eight of twelve.
