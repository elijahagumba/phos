# 106. All three on one page

*The stack misses the handoff. The page of eight keeps half of those. ADR 0311.*

Two pairs of tags, two slow walks, and eight handoffs. The stack catches the pairs
and the walks, four in all, and misses every handoff.

Put the handoffs on the same page. The earlier four stay. Four handoffs fit in the
slots that are left, and four do not. The misses go from eight to four. A single
ordinary write still adds nothing.

This is a made example, not a plant. Version 1 still stands at eight of twelve.
