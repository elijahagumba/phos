# 71 — A signal we do not trust yet

Sentiel could see only one of the two insiders in a public dataset. A careful look at
the second one, day by day, found what gave him away: he kept going back to job sites
nobody else at the company ever visited.

Counting those returns finds both insiders and nobody else. That sounds like a
breakthrough, and we are not calling it one. In this simulated company, no ordinary
person ever goes back to a site nobody else uses, and real people do that all the time.
So the signal may be spotting how the dataset was made, not how people behave.

It will be tested once on a larger release it has never seen, with a check for exactly
that question written down first.
