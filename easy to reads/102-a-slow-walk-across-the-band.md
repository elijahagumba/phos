# 102. A slow walk across the band

*One tag, seven hundred and twenty small steps, and the joint check sees nothing. ADR 0307.*

Two tags moving together are already one move. One tag is not. If that one tag is
walked from 6.5 toward 7.5 in 720 steps, a second apart, every step stays inside the
ordinary band and the joint check stays quiet.

Seven hundred and twenty steps are one walk, and the time shown is the first step.
Seven hundred and nineteen are not a walk. A two-second gap splits it, and neither
piece is long enough. Twelve such walks cannot all fit on a page of eight, and the
ones left off are at most half of the twelve the joint check misses.

This is a made example, not a plant. The sealed walk recording stays unopened.
Version 1 still stands at eight of twelve.
