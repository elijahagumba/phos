# 122. The front door names the three standings

*Held, broken, or unstated. Only a broken law takes a slot. ADR 0325.*

The first paragraph of the repository now says what the operator page does.
A plant names the laws in force. Each named law is held, broken, or
unstated. Only a broken law takes a slot. The page holds at most eight.
The order is time, then the law's name. Every sentence names its law, then
the fact. A score is refused.

A break is a false law. The page does not name the cause. Held means the
check ran and the law stood. Unstated means the plant named the law and
supplied nothing that law can be checked against. An empty register checks
nothing.

The older detectors are still in the repository. They are how the published
measurements can be rebuilt. They do not enter this page.

Nothing new was scored. Version 1 stays at eight of twelve. This is not a
result about a plant.
