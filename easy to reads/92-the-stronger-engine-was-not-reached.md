# 92. The stronger engine was not reached

*The four-lane programme is closed. ADR 0297.*

The aim was an engine clearly stronger than the one already shipped. One change was kept. It
can see who is giving orders, on a made-up plant, and an attacker at the operator's own desk
still gets past it.

Two changes measured a gain and were turned down. One caught more and arrived later. One got
quieter and caught fewer. A fourth, looking at the instruments together, caught fewer than the
engine we already have: 15 against 16, on the development runs.

The package was not built. The one look at the old recordings was not taken. The empty record
numbers are not a to-do list.

There is no new plan in this note. The next question, if there is one, gets written down
before it is measured. Version 1 still stands at eight of twelve.
