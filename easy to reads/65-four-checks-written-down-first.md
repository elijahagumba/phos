# 65 — Four checks, written down first

The 4 October detector caught 53 of 58 attacks on the 2022 edition and 36 of 52
on the 2023 edition. Four checks on that score are written down, and none has
been run.

One asks what 8 random alerts a shift would catch, 500 times, from a fixed seed.
One asks which measurement produced each catch, and whether the range or the
plant-wide prediction was the larger score. One scores the two controls that
were frozen with the detector, byte for byte. One asks whether the two published
rows were actually two different alert lists.

Nothing in the detector changes while those questions are open.
