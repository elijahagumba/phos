# 114. Only a broken law takes a slot

*Held and unstated are listed. They take no place. ADR 0319.*

A law the plant has named has one of three standings. Held means the facts
were there and the law was satisfied. Broken means the law was false.
Unstated means the law is named and there was nothing to check it against. A
missing list of writes is unstated.

Only a broken law is placed on the operator page. Held and unstated stay on
their own lists. They do not use one of the eight places. A law the plant did
not name is on none of the three lists.

There is no score, no weight, and no probability on any of the three. The
time of a fact is recorded for a break. A held law has no clock on it, so it
cannot be lined up against the breaks. The old detector files are unchanged.
Nothing was scored on a plant. Version 1 still stands at eight of twelve.
This page is not that result.
