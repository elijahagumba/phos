# 99. More incidents in the same eight

*Repeated alerts of one incident were using up the page. ADR 0304.*

Eight places, five incidents. Three of the incidents shout three times each, so the
loud ones fill the page and two quieter incidents never appear. That is two misses.

If the repeats of one incident count once, and the time shown is the first of them,
all five fit. The misses go from two to none. The page does not grow past eight.
Two different incidents that happen a second apart stay two incidents. The old
grouper is not changed.

This is a made example, not a plant. Version 1 still stands at eight of twelve.
