# 113. An undeclared law is silence

*A plant names the laws that are in force. ADR 0318.*

A law is checked only when the plant has named it. A law the plant did not
name is not checked, and it cannot become a slot. A page that is handed a
slot for a name the plant did not declare refuses that slot.

An empty list of laws is silence. The same steps can hold a joint move, a
walk of the length already fixed, and a program change with nothing booked
over it. With no law named, the page stays empty.

Naming only the joint move shows that move. The walk on the same steps stays
quiet, because that law was not named.

The four laws already written are still the only ones. The old detector files
are unchanged. Nothing was scored on a plant. Version 1 still stands at eight
of twelve. This page is not that result.
