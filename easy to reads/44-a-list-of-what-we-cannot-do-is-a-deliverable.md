# 44 — A list of what we cannot do is a deliverable

PHOS has a growing file whose only job is to say what the system **will not claim**, why, and
what would have to arrive from the world before that sentence could move. That file is the
refusal register. It is not a graveyard of excuses. Read down the third column and it is a
shopping list for a design partner.

This note is the end-of-phase check on that list, not a new measurement.

## What was already true

Twenty-six entries. Six of them were known before this phase started: maintenance and an
attacker still look the same without a work order; peer-relative insider detection has no
labelled case; cross-plant generalisation is not earned; synthetic data cannot prove operational
usefulness; the Atlas queue cannot be ranked; there is still no labelled public command
capture.

The rest were written as U1 through U5 found them. Atlas's false alarms on HAI are a short fit,
not a fact about the plant. Sentiel cannot hear a behaviour that moves fewer than fourteen
features at once. The second CERT insider's motive is in the corpus as job-board hosts and a
competitor's inbox, and the feature space counts *how many* hosts rather than *which*. A
synthetic plant can now host the situations HAI could not show, and the answers still cannot
travel. An alert can be read without a probability. A study without plant-literate subjects is
not a field result.

Every entry already named what would lift it.

## What this pass caught

A confirmation pass is only worth running if it is allowed to find drift. Four entries had
some.

The cross-plant lift still asked for a declared physical-role mapping. That mapping was
already measured: train on BATADAL, score HAI, and the transfer is **louder** than HAI scored
against itself. The claim stays refused; the lift is now a transfer that is not louder, or a
third process that actually generalises.

Two lift columns still named U5 as if plant-literate operators might arrive there. U5 ran. It
ran a mechanical check with **zero** human subjects. Recruiting them is the next item, which
this pass does not start.

And one evidence line said the second insider's largest shift on any of the 34 features was
z = 1.07. That was a count-feature illustration copied forward. The report the line cites ranks
by absolute z and leads with **1.27**. The 1.07 figure is still true of removable-device
connects. It is not the largest of the thirty-four.

The other twenty-two entries matched the code.

## What the phase actually earned

Diagnosis of both engines. A synthetic plant that can host the missing situations. A
plain-English alert. An instrument for a human study that has not been sat. A map of the
boundary, confirmed current.

What it did not earn: a quieter Atlas, a Sentiel that can hear the second insider, a field
result, or permission to start the product surface. Two levers — a calibrator that does not
need labelled positives, and an aggregation that is not capped at one per feature — sit in a
waiting room labelled *not authorised*. Outreach is still a human action.

The next item writes a two-week question a plant could say yes to. It is not this note.

## The one sentence to keep

PHOS now has a current list of what it cannot do. Four lines on that list had gone stale and
were rewritten rather than left looking finished. Nothing on it is a product claim.
