# 42 — An alert you can read without a probability

U1 told us Atlas's false alarms were the model's. U2 told us Sentiel's misses were an aggregation
ceiling. U3 built twelve situations where those stories can be exercised. None of that was yet
something a person could *read* and act on.

U4 is the smallest thing that turns an existing narrative into plain English. It is not a
dashboard, not a website, and not the product UI. It takes the story the ontology already holds and
prints four things, in order:

1. What happened, in one short paragraph, with the evidence.
2. An assessment — including **unresolved** when two explanations cannot be split.
3. The single most useful next fact that would settle it, named specifically.
4. The full competing set behind an explicit "why", for anyone who wants the machinery.

No confidence figure appears. If the only thing that would separate two explanations is a prior
written into a template, the assessment is unresolved: a prior is not knowledge.

## The Sentiel page says what it cannot see

Gate 2 required this in the output itself, not only in an ADR. When the story is Sentiel's, the
render ends with a known limit: at the current 48-alert operating point at least 14 of 34 features
must speak at once for any entity-day to alert, and 31 of one labelled insider's 42 malicious days
were structurally unalertable for that reason. That is a diagnosed ceiling of the aggregation, not
a gap in the data. A reader of the alert knows what the ranking cannot currently reach.

## What the ontology could not carry alone

The narrative already holds what happened and the competing explanations. Naming the *next* fact
needs the template library's support/contradict asymmetries. Atlas can ask for a covering work
order or a write from a device that has never commanded this target. Sentiel's templates declare
no contradictory patterns at all, so the honest ask is a declared sanction record — and the
admission that no corpus PHOS reads carries one.

## What this is not

Not Intelligence. Not FastAPI. Not React. Not permission to surface an uncalibrated 82%. U5 built the three-arm instrument and ran a labelled non-operator check (note 43). That check is not a field study.
