# 55 — The liar is named before it is run

One of the things v1 is for is an attack that lies to the operator: the readings look calm,
and the plant is not. The pass criteria already ask whether two explanations can be told
apart. This is the narrower question, and it is fixed before anyone runs it.

The runs are the liar attack, on the three simulated plants that already have one, at every
step of the existing difficulty dial, including the subtle steps and the near-invisible one.
The seeds are 94001 through 94020. Those seeds have not been used to build the library or
the demo. That is 360 runs.

More than half of them have to come out the same way. Atlas's single leading explanation
has to be that the instrument is what failed, and it has to cite either a command that the
plant did not follow or two signals that no longer agree. A tie does not count. Saying
"not enough to tell" does not count. The words in the paragraph do not count; the citation
has to be the evidence's own label.

Nothing here is a result. The runs have not been done.

---

**The short version.** The seeds and the pass mark for the lying-readings test are written
down first. More than half of 360 runs, and no result yet.
