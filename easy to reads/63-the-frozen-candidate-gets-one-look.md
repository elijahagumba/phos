# 63 — The frozen candidate gets one look, written down first

The detector that grouped its own alarms, and then predicted each sensor from
the rest of the plant, was finished on 4 October. The two later HAI editions
were not on disk yet, so none of its settings was chosen by looking at them.

Grouped Atlas has already had its one look at those editions, and it loses to a
grouped range check. The open question is whether this older detector beats
that range check.

The look is a named command. It will score the detector twice on the same
alarms: once with the grouping it carries inside itself, and once with the
shared grouping every other detector uses. The range check and Atlas stay at
the counts already published. The command has not been run.
