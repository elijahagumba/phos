# The build names its seeds before it looks

**What this is:** a promise about files that have not been opened yet.

**Why it exists:** a test you have already peeked at is not a test. ADR 0144 writes the split
down first.

## Two piles, neither of them scored

Think of a teacher who writes the exam questions, seals half of them, and only then opens the
textbook. The October build does that with seeds and with files.

The seeds it may practise on are 97001 to 97020. The sealed packet is 97101 to 97120. Neither
packet is one of the old ones: not the twenty liar seeds, not the arena, not the three
confirmation batches, not the ICSSim runs. A test checks that the piles do not share a number.

Kelmarsh, a real English wind farm, arrives as yearly bundles that mix all six turbines. The
open pile is 2016–2022. 2023, 2024, and the meter archives that do not say which year they are,
stay sealed. Penmanshiel arrives already split by the publisher: turbines 1–10 (there is no
turbine 3) are open, turbines 11–15 are sealed. Both are CC BY 4.0 on the Zenodo record. A count
of false alerts there would be about those turbines. It would not be a claim that PHOS catches
attacks.

The 4SICS lab captures are three days. The first two are open, the third is sealed. They were
not downloaded. The page asks for credit and a link. An email permission is recorded as stated;
the email itself is not in the repository.

GRFICS, a simulated chemical plant, is allowed as a separate testbed. Its code is GPL-3.0 and
stays in its own box. PHOS keeps only the data that box writes. Ten practice runs and ten sealed
runs are named, including which sensor is lying. None of them has been generated. Fortiphyd asked
for credit.

## What this note is not

No score. No catch rate. The scorecard — catches inside the budget, the gap over a simple rival
and over chance, whether the lying sensor is named, how far the reconstructed value is, whether
maintenance alerts close, calibration, false alerts per shift, and how long it took — is a list
of headings with the numbers still blank.

The TU Wien factory is not in this pile. It gets its own declaration, on the lane that will use
it, before anyone opens it.
