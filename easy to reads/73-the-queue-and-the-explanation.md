# 73 — The queue, and the explanation

Three measurements landed together. Each one had been scored once, on seeds named
beforehand. The bars they were given still stand.

The queue. No new ordering beat the one Atlas already uses. On the 2020 HAI file an
oracle that already knows which alerts are real can only get to 21 of 38. Most of the
misses are shifts that contain more attacks than front-of-queue slots, so they cannot
all go first. That limit belongs to the way the score is defined. The standing result
on the later files stays 57 of 203. The look that would reopen those files was written
down and not taken.

The explanation. On a fresh batch of the same five hard cases, 60 of 60 contentful
answers were the right one, where the previous batch was 33 of 54. Same kinds of case,
new runs. That is not a test of a kind of case the separators were not built for. The
confidence test is still not met. Its "high" mark is a statistical lower bound on a
small group of answers that share one count, and that bound cannot reach the line it
was given. That is a fault in the mark, not a finding that the answers were unsure.

The trusted station. A hostile write from the station the plant already trusts still
does not win: 0 of 20. The new facts do show up on every one of those writes, and on
none of the ordinary unusual ones. They stay too light, under the fixed weight, to
beat "we do not know yet." Who sent the write was not loosened, and the weights were
not turned. This is a plant we built. It is not the frozen 0 of 16.
