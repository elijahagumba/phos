# 70 — Quiet on years it never saw

Atlas watched six real wind turbines for 2023 and 2024, two years it had never seen,
and was scored once. It raised 29 alerts in two years. The turbines' own logs
explained 23 of them. That matches what it did on the four years before, so the
quiet was not luck.

Two things did not work. On harder simulated cases, like a fault during maintenance,
its best explanation was right 33 times in 54, and it gave right and wrong answers the
same confidence, so that number stays hidden. And on real network traffic from a
security lab, most messages were Siemens S7, which Atlas cannot read yet.
