# The stamps that public files cannot give

**What this is:** Session C0 of the category-defining programme. We kept the product.
We stopped treating public ZIP numbers as the pass/fail for the cycle.

**Why it exists:** "2–3 pages a day," "70% of attack windows," and "10× quieter than
per-signal baselines on every dataset" are what you would want from a staffed plant.
The public files are not that plant. Leaving those sentences in the lock trained every
agent in this repo to chase figures the files cannot honestly give.

## Two lists, not one

Think of a school exam written for a working factory, then sat by a lab notebook.

On **public files**, success is telling the truth: a table of joint vs univariate
(8.6× here, 6.6× there — not a 10× stamp), a refused 2–3/day on distillation even
after the recipe log was joined, command ranking still 0/16 and 1/4 scored apart,
CERT still 10% precision at 48 tickets. Silence when the caption is missing.

On a **design partner**, success is a characterised queue. Two or three pages a day
is the conversation with them, not a gate on a ZIP we already measured as loud.

The speech contract did not get softer. Competing explanations, reconstructable
evidence, and the right to stay quiet are still the thing. Known-station writes still
look like maintenance. We still do not print an uncalibrated "82% sure."

## What started then

The live session after C0 was **C1**: write down, in tests, what today's joint
model actually assumes. That landed (note 22, ADR 0050). Saturated FP load as a
refuse landed next (note 23, ADR 0051). Clock A then ran C3–Ω2 (notes 24–27).
The live session is now **P1**, blocked on a design partner.

The plan is docs/category-defining-roadmap.md (lab record, not published).
The decision is [ADR 0049](../docs/adr/0049-split-scorecard-public-files-vs-partner.md).

## What this is not

- Not live-plant ready.
- Not a claim that 70% or 2–3/day was earned.
- Not permission to retune templates or invert ranking.
- Not Intelligence, API, or UI.
