# 95. A kept day halves what is left

*The bar for a day of work on Atlas. Nothing here has been measured. ADR 0300.*

The aim is a real gain every day. A day counts when the new detector catches at least half
of the attacks the current one still misses, inside the same eight alerts a shift, and
without raising the false alarms. On an attack both of them catch, the new one must not
notice later.

Do that five days in a row and one thirty-second of those misses remain. That is the
whole of the ambition, written as arithmetic so it cannot be redefined after a table
comes back. It has not happened. A day that misses the bar is written down, and that
idea is not tried again the next day.

The two engines already kept are the ones a day has to beat: the shipped detector, and
the one that learned which stations speak. Whichever catches more of the day's attacks
is the one the half is taken from. An attack that looks exactly like authorised
maintenance stays out of that count. The recordings already used stay used. A sealed
test waits until two days have actually been kept.

Version 1 still stands at eight of twelve. The first test, the command that stays inside
an ordinary setpoint, is the next note.
