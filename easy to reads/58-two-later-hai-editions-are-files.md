# 58 — Two later HAI editions are files, and nothing has been scored on them

HAI 22.04 and 23.05 were pointers in the download. The publisher's account for large
files is over its budget, and the usual download still returns the pointer rather than
the file. The files themselves were available from the same commit. Each checksum
matches the pointer. They are on disk, and they are not in git.

They are the editions that have not been used to design anything. No detector has been
scored on them. That wait is the point: when a grouped setting is tried, these two
files get one look.
